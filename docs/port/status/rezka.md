## Deviations candidates

The V2 `_submit` sets its busy flag after asynchronous USB detection; the unconnected controller sets it before detection to prevent duplicate submissions. It also rejects explicit `ok: false` from prepare, printer or confirm instead of declaring success, and blocks further submissions after an uncertain network or physical-print outcome until reconciled. V2 ignores the native `PrintService.printRps` result and may confirm even when `ok` is false; this is deliberately not copied. The new flow also checks prepared output count, print flags and required EPCs before physical printing, while V2 checks the count only after printing and skips empty EPCs. These safety differences must remain explicit and be reviewed with the user/integrator.
